import { Card, CardGrid } from '@astrojs/starlight/components';

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Light and shadows" icon="star">
    Lamps in the colour and brightness of the light entity, the sun of `sun.sun` with its shadows, and shadows kept within what the device can draw.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Doors, covers and more" icon="puzzle">
    Doors that swing or slide, covers that roll up or turn their slats, objects that turn, a TV that lights the room, people tracked by a radar.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Rooms and alarms" icon="warning">
    Rooms coloured by temperature, humidity, CO₂ or any sensor; a room that blinks with smoke or a water leak, and the camera going there.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Sky, weather and boxes" icon="sun">
    A sky that follows the sun, stars and the moon at night, the forecast, and boxes in the corners for what is on, energy and people.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

This card is a fork of [adizanni/floor3d-card](https://github.com/adizanni/floor3d-card), with the same `custom:floor3d-card` and the same configuration: a card that worked with the original works with this one.
